    How are you using this site?

    What's their ZIP code?

    We use it to find programs near them and sort them by distance. We cover Connecticut, Massachusetts and New York so far.

    When were they born?

    Some programs open or close at certain ages. With a birthdate we can tell you the exact date each one opens.

    What level of support do they need?

    This is the autism level written in their diagnosis report (Level 1, 2 or 3). We use it to put programs that fit their needs first, such as 24-hour residential programs and job coaching for higher support needs.

    That's fine. It's usually on the report from the doctor or psychologist who made the diagnosis. We'll show everything in the usual order.

    Do they get SSI (Supplemental Security Income)?

    SSI is a monthly payment from Social Security. It changes how much they keep when they work and what they pay for housing.
