Research log

Changes that affect how families should read this
  • Agency renames. The state housing agency DHCD is now EOHLC (2023). The Massachusetts Rehabilitation Commission (MRC) is now MassAbility (2024). Some mass.gov pages still use the old names.
  • Section 8 lists. EOHLC's statewide Section 8 mobile-voucher list has been closed since Jan 13, 2025, and Boston Housing Authority's Section 8 list is closed. The MassNAHRO Centralized Waiting List and Cambridge Housing Authority lists were open when checked.
  • Autism without intellectual disability. These adults can qualify for DDS (since the 2014 Autism Omnibus law), but DDS's published service list for them does not include 24-hour residential care.
  • Federal WOTC hiring credit lapsed on Dec 31, 2025. Massachusetts's own Disability Employment Tax Credit is still active.
Left out because they closed or are out of state
  • Breaking Grounds Café, Peabody (Northeast Arc), closed Aug 1, 2026.
  • INspire Café, Wakefield, closed Sept 5, 2026.
  • Bitty & Beau's Coffee, Melrose, closed 2023.
  • Rising Tide Car Wash is in Florida. No Massachusetts location.
  • Massachusetts Autism Waiver covers children 0–9 only, with no employment or housing part.
  • MassAccess Housing Registry was replaced in 2022 by Housing Navigator MA and MyMassHome.
  • Not yet verified: EY Neuro-Diverse Center (Boston), Advocates/HMEA Neurodiversity @ Work, an unnamed inclusive café that opened in Boston in March 2026, Lowell Commission on Disability.
Still open
  • Maximum subsidy amounts for MRVP and AHVP are not published.
  • Room-and-board for MassHealth Group Adult Foster Care and costs at private residential programs (only Berkshire Hills Music Academy publishes tuition).
  • Big-company neurodiversity hiring programs in Boston could not be confirmed as active.
  • Specialized Housing, Inc. still needs confirming on its own website. Newton Housing Authority's site could not be reached.
  • Market rents by city are not yet in the data. The planner needs them for the no-subsidy scenario.
